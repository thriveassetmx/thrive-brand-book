# Messaging

Logos, colour and type are covered elsewhere in this brand book. This file
records the **words** — taglines, descriptor lines and the credential line —
so they stop living only on printed cards.

Source: business cards and pull-up banner, transcribed 2026-09-01. Where a
website says something different, that difference is noted rather than silently
reconciled.

## Thrive Property MX

| Slot | Line |
|---|---|

| Descriptor | **EXCELLENCE IN MANAGEMENT & OPERATIONS** |
| Descriptor, short | **MANAGEMENT & OPERATIONS** *(banner)* |
| Short domain | **THRIVE.RENT** |

The website runs a different set, aimed at each audience rather than at the
brand:

- **Love Where You Live** — closing line on the homepage and About page
- **Find Your Next Home** — renters, under the eyebrow "LOOKING TO RENT?"
- **Grow Your Portfolio** — owners, under "OWN A PROPERTY?"
- **Rent with us** — recurring call to action

Supporting claims from the About page, useful anywhere the brand needs proof
rather than a slogan:

- One team, not five vendors.
- Veteran-owned, mission-driven.
- Milwaukee, not a national portfolio.

## Shared

Both cards close with the same credential line:

> A Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business

## How the two taglines relate

They are built as a pair, and should stay one:

- **Property MX** *runs* the standard — one crew, one standard.
- **Asset MX** *is* the standard behind that operation.

Rewriting either line alone breaks the pairing.

## Open items

- **Spelling.** The Property MX site renders the tagline as "One Crew. One
  **Standart**" in the `<h1>` of `/about-us/`. The collateral spelling —
  *Standard* — is correct; the site needs fixing.
- **Positioning conflict.** Property MX says "Milwaukee, not a national
  portfolio" while Asset MX describes itself as "a national commercial real
  estate firm". That split is defensible for a subsidiary and a parent, but the
  two should not be pitched side by side without acknowledging it.
- **Logo generation.** The transcribed collateral uses the older lockup — pale
  blue wordmark with a yellow rule — not the current full-colour logo in this
  brand book. Check before reprinting.
